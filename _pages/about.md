Hi! I am Atri Vivek Sharma, a PhD student at Imperial College London supervised by [Prof. Alessio Lomuscio](https://www.doc.ic.ac.uk/~alessio/) at the [SAIL Lab](https://sail.doc.ic.ac.uk). My research focuses on the safety of Machine Learning algorithms, specifically regarding robustness and interpretability in safety-critical domains. My current work examines the robustness of Large Language Models within agentic settings, with a focus on mitigating intrinsic hallucinations. Beyond my PhD, I am developing specification-driven evaluations for AI Agents at [Safe Intelligence](https://safeintelligence.ai). My previous research involved enhancing the adversarial robustness of tabular data models like XGBoost.

Prior to my PhD, I worked at Pangaea Data, a London-based startup leveraging AI in healthcare. There, I led the development of an NLP-driven product pipeline designed to identify at-risk or undiagnosed patients with specific diseases. My experience in applying AI to healthcare underscored the urgent need for more reliable and interpretable ML systems that experts can trust for critical decision-making.

Outside of research, I love traveling and am currently training for my first marathon!
